## Problem statements
1. **Product Line Performance & Satisfaction** — mean Rating and gross income by
   Product line (grouped summaries, two one-way ANOVAs; both ns at 5%).
2. **Geographical & Branch Sales Comparison** — revenue and Member/Normal mix by
   Branch/City (ANOVA ns; chi-square independence ns).
3. **Payment Method Demographics** — Payment × Gender and Payment × Customer type
   cross-tabs with shares (both chi-squares ns).
4. **Peak Shopping Hours & Days** — hourly, part-of-day and weekday volume/revenue
   (goodness-of-fit uniformity tests ns; peaks are descriptive).
5. **Profitability & Pricing Elasticity** — Unit price vs Quantity (r = 0.011, ns)
   and vs gross income (r = 0.634, invoice arithmetic); profitability by line.

## Interpretation cautions
- `Total = 1.05 × Quantity × Unit price` for every row; `Tax 5% = gross income =
  0.05 × cogs`; `gross margin percentage` is constant. Regressions on these
  columns demonstrate model fitting, not causal discoveries.
- Branch and City correspond one-to-one.
- Dates cover only 1 January to 30 March 2019; hours only 10:00–20:00.
- Analysis is observational; inferences are associative, never causal.
